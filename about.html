About Us

BSIT-1G Student Hub was created in 2026 as a group project for our Introduction to Computing class at Davao del Norte State College.
We are three Bachelor of Science in Information Technology students from Section 1G, and this website is our way of showing
what we have learned about building and publishing a website.

Our Goals

  • Practice writing clean HTML and CSS
  • Work together as a team
  • Learn how to test and publish website

What We Learned

  • How to organize website into different connected HTML pages
  • How to style every page with only one CSS file
  • How to make a page readable and usable for everyone

Our School

We are students of Davao del Norte State College in Panabo City. As part of our BSIT course, we built this website for our
Introduction to Computing class to practice what we have learned about HTML, CSS, and publishing a site online.
